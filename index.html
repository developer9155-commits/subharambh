About the Freshers Party

Subharambh means "a good beginning". This freshers party is organised by the Diploma students of Centurion University to welcome first-year students and bring every year together for an evening of music, dance, fashion and fun.

The Chief Guests will select Mr. Fresher and Mrs. Fresher. Any student can take part in the performances by registering below.
